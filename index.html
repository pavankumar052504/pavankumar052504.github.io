Background & Philosophy

I’m Pavan Kumar — a data analyst who believes every spreadsheet is a story waiting to be told. I completed my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science & Engineering at HITS, Hyderabad, where I built a rock-solid foundation in programming, databases, algorithms and problem-solving — the same engineering mindset I now bring to every dataset.

Where most people see rows and columns, I see patterns, risks and hidden opportunities. With SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau and Excel, I dig into messy data, clean it until it’s trustworthy, and transform it into dashboards and insights that make decision-makers say, “now that makes sense.”

My approach is simple: engineer-grade accuracy, designer-level clarity, and a relentless focus on business impact. No vanity charts. No noise. Just numbers that lead to action.

“Data is just noise until someone gives it meaning — I turn that noise into decisions.”
